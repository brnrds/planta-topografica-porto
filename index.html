Old map of Porto (1892)

This interactive Porto map presents the Planta Topográfica da Cidade do Porto of 1892 — a detailed old map of Oporto (Porto, Portugal) drawn at 1:500 scale under Augusto Gerardo Teles Ferreira for the Câmara Municipal do Porto. It is widely regarded as the first rigorous cadastral survey of the city, recording streets, building footprints, and relief at the end of the nineteenth century.

If you are looking for a historical map of Porto, a vintage map of Oporto, or a high-resolution 1892 Porto map, this deep-zoom viewer lets you explore the full survey without losing detail — from the Douro riverfront and Ribeira to the expanding urban fabric of the period.

How to use this map

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom into streets and individual blocks.
  • Click and drag to pan across the city.
  • Use the on-screen controls for home view, full screen, and rotation.

Historical context

Earlier plans of Porto include George Balck’s 1813 Planta Redonda and the 1833 Society for the Diffusion of Useful Knowledge engraving of Oporto. The 1892 topographic survey post-dates those and offers far greater urban detail. The original sheets are preserved at the Porto Municipal Archive and are also accessible through the municipality’s Cartas Históricas Interativas service.
