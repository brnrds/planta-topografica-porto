Porto map and Oporto map — what you are looking at

If you searched for a Porto map, Oporto map, old Porto map, or vintage map of Porto, this survey is one of the most detailed nineteenth-century plans of the city. Commissioned by the Câmara Municipal do Porto for urban planning, it records the built fabric, street network, and relief of Porto at the end of the 1800s at 1:500 scale — the first rigorous cadastral mapping of the city.

The original documents are held at the Porto Municipal Archive (Gisa). This viewer digitises and stitches those sheets into a single navigable deep-zoom historical map, so you can inspect blocks, quays, and landmarks without losing resolution.

How to use the viewer

  • Scroll or pinch to zoom into streets and building footprints.
  • Drag to pan across the full 1892 city extent.
  • Use on-screen controls for home view, full screen, and rotation.

Other historical maps of Porto

Earlier plans include George Balck’s 1813 Planta Redonda and the 1833 SDUK engraving of Oporto. The 1892 Teles Ferreira survey post-dates those and offers far greater urban detail — it is the reference cartography for late-nineteenth-century Porto.

Who made the 1892 map?

Where does the imagery come from?
Source documents published by the Câmara Municipal do Porto.
